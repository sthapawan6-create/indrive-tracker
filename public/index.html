Account Grouping & Reporting Guide

To ensure "Receivables" and "Payables" reporting is accurate, follow these grouping conventions:

1. Bank & Cash

Any account where the Group name contains bank or cash (case-insensitive) will be treated as liquid assets. These are excluded from the Party Receivables/Payables list.

Example Group: "Nabil Bank", "Petty Cash"

2. Receivables (लिनु पर्ने)

Calculated from accounts where:

  • Type is Asset and balance is Debit (Dr)
  • Type is Liability and balance is Debit (Dr) - (Advance to suppliers)
  • Group does NOT contain "bank" or "cash"

3. Payables (दिनु पर्ने)

Calculated from accounts where:

  • Type is Liability and balance is Credit (Cr)
  • Type is Asset and balance is Credit (Cr) - (Overdrawn accounts)
  • Group does NOT contain "bank" or "cash"

4. Net Worth Logic

Net Worth = (Liquid + Receivables + Revenue) - (Payables + Expense)

Note: Bike Tracker expenses are automatically included in Total Expenses.
